Keys in one box are typed one after another; Ctrl+B means hold Ctrl and press B. Config: ~/.config/nvim Inside herdr or tmux, Ctrl+B is their prefix: use Space e, then q to hide the sidebar. If Ctrl+/ does nothing, use gcc.
